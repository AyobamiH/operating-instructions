# Editorial policy

This is the canonical *content-strategy guidance*, not a publishing command or a replacement for platform-specific account permissions. A scheduled publisher must explicitly load this policy at generation time to rely on it.

## Positioning

**AI-generated code is not the outcome; dependable software, independently verified in real use, is the outcome.**

Demonstrate this through real engineering evidence rather than repeating the slogan in every post. Preserve the distinction between verified production results, partial tests, open acceptance and unproven claims.

## Content pillars

1. Reliable AI agents and automation — state, permissions, MCP integrations, durable execution and recovery.
2. Production engineering and recovery — incidents, root causes, safe retries, observability, deployment and verification.
3. Open-source contributions and lessons — maintainers, real fixes, reproducible evidence and credible collaboration.
4. Commercially useful software — customer problems, working solutions, product decisions and outcomes.

Pillars are a starting hypothesis, not a rigid quota. Adapt them to actual audience questions, research and evidence.

## Evidence-to-content workflow

For each useful incident or contribution, capture:
- The problem and why it mattered.
- What was attempted, including failed or incomplete approaches.
- Established root cause versus hypotheses.
- What actually changed, with version, environment and date.
- Tests, independent readback and remaining gaps.
- Reusable lesson and the audience it serves.
- Source links and what may be shared publicly.

Never fabricate incidents, customers, metrics, revenue, endorsements, merge status or production results. Check that private data, tokens, confidential client information and sensitive operational details are excluded.

## Channel-specific execution

- **LinkedIn:** clear professional framing, real engineering lessons, useful demonstrations and relevance to employers, collaborators and buyers. Do not turn every post into a pitch.
- **Reddit:** answer the community's actual question first. Respect subreddit rules, disclose relevant affiliations and avoid repetitive self-promotion or manufactured engagement.
- **Portfolio and articles:** link to durable proof, explain context and limitations, and provide a useful path to enquiry or collaboration.
- **Other platforms:** adapt format and tone without changing factual claims or verification status.

Do not infer that a global ChatGPT custom-instruction change rewrites queued, scheduled or already-published posts. Content-generation workers should load an explicit version of this file; publishing, scheduling and provider readback remain separate operational concerns.

## Commercial relevance

Consider focused audits, working prototypes and useful technical insights when they solve a prospect's real problem. Keep speculative opportunities separate from demonstrated demand. Avoid unsupported earnings claims or promises of customer acquisition.

## Measurement

Evaluate meaningful outcomes such as qualified conversations, profile visits with intent, enquiries, customers, credible referrals and revenue. Impressions and post counts are supporting diagnostics, not success by themselves. Keep account, platform and campaign measurements separate.

## Infrastructure

Reuse the existing knowledge and publishing systems. Keep canonical evidence and editorial policy portable and independently maintained. Do not create another scheduling or content-production system simply to apply this policy.
